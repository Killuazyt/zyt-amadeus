Dynamic linking and replacement
-------------------------------
Qt, PySide6, and Shiboken are distributed as separate DLL/PYD/plugin files under
the installed application's _internal\PySide6 and _internal\shiboken6 paths.
They are not statically linked into Amadeus.exe. A recipient may replace those
files with ABI-compatible, user-built Qt for Python 6.11.1 libraries for the
same Windows x64 and CPython 3.11 ABI. The installer and application impose no
signature check or technical restriction on such replacement.

You may reverse engineer the combined work to debug modifications to the LGPL
libraries. This permission is not limited by the project license or installer.
Keep a recoverable copy of the installed directory before replacing libraries.
